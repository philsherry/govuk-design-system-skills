## What this video shows

The most fundamental GOV.UK design pattern — one thing per page. Covers question page structure, the five core elements, and the `isPageHeading` technique that makes the form label and the page heading the same element.

## What you will see

1. The one-thing-per-page principle and when exceptions apply
2. The five core elements of a question page
3. Making the label or legend the page heading with `isPageHeading`
4. A code example showing a complete question page
5. Accessibility, do's and don'ts, and the ten related components

## What you will need

- Copy `workspace.example.yaml` to `workspace.yaml`: `cp workspace.example.yaml workspace.yaml`
- Clone `govuk-design-system-skills` into `workspace/`: `npx -y degit philsherry/govuk-design-system-skills workspace/govuk-design-system-skills`
